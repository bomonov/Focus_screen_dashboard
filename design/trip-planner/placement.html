Where should trips live?

The top of the Focus Screen at about real proportions, on a sample afternoon: you're at the gym, class is done for the day, so it's planning home. The new part has a dashed teal outline. The sample stops and times are made up. Click the one you like.

A

A pill of its own

Its own pill in the top-bar row, like Next class, Bus and Weather. It shows when to leave and the first bus. Hold it and a glass card drops down with the whole trip and a couple of other options. Before a class it plans to class; after your last class it plans home.

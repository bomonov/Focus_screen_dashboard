Adding a missing verse: three ways

The Color-Code Lyrics window, with a made-up song whose lyrics skip verse 2. In all three, the added lines show on the Focus Screen like any other line, get colored the same way, and are timed with the same tap timing (J). Click the one that feels easiest, then tell me in the chat.

A · Paste between two lines

Hover between any two lines and a ＋ shows. Also on the right-click menu: “Add lines after this one…”.

You pick the spot in the list. The new lines sit between their neighbours with no times yet (they're spread over the gap so you can see them), and the first tap on each line puts it where it's sung.

While the verse plays, press ＋ Add lines at 1:24 (or ⌘N) and paste. The first line starts right where you pressed; the rest share out the time up to the next line, then tap J through them to put each in place.

You find the spot by ear, where the verse is actually sung. The lines come in already close to where they belong, so a tap is fixing, not placing.

C · Edit the song's words as text

1“Edit words…” turns the line list into the song's text. Paste the verse in the gap, fix any wrong word.

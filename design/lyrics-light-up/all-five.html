What to watch for, on the right

1. Each word pops (rise, swell, glow) as it lights.
2. The Japanese guess times each piece by how long it sounds: ありがとう and 永遠に now get the same time, since both are five beats.
3. The romaji lights up left to right with the line, quietly, without popping.
4. The held "tonight" swells letter by letter as the light reaches each one.
5. The light's edge is softer, so it flows from word to word.
